Pass the Ember. A human and AI guessing game powered by Muse Ember.
One player describes a secret place, without saying its name. Muse checks the prompt and turns the description into a three dimensional drawing.
We begin in San Francisco. The models only receive the picture. Each locks a guess, then explains the visual clues behind it.
Next, Norway. The places get harder. The same picture can lead different models to very different answers.
Finally, Nepal. More intricate architecture, fewer familiar landmarks. Luna, Terra, Sonnet and Haiku take turns describing and guessing.
People can join too. You have one minute to write, draw and refine. Then the answer is revealed and the points are shared.
Pass the Ember. What can you make them see?

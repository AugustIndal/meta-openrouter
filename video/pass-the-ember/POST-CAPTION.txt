Pass the Ember 🔥
One player describes a secret place. Muse Ember checks the prompt and turns words into a 3D scene. Everyone else has to guess from the picture.

60 seconds to refine your drawing. Humans and AI at the same table. Three increasingly difficult rounds: San Francisco → Norway → Nepal.

Built for the Meta × OpenRouter hackathon.

This demo uses actual model outputs and game results. Waiting is sped up; prompt-entry animations are labeled replays. Model explanations summarize visible evidence, not private internal reasoning.

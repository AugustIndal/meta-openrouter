Subjective visual review of all 36 original initial-camera previews at 1200x800 via six 3 × 2 contact sheets. Scores 0-10 combine recognizable shape, fidelity to the supplied physical description, and composition. 0 means no rendered output;1 means renderer works but subject is hidden.5-6 means recognizable but visibly flawed;7-8 means clear and reasonably composed;9-10 reserved for excellent geometry and finish. No scene was manually repaired. These are exploratory, unblinded ratings with one sample per setting.
